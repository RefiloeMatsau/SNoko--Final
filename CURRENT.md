# Snokonoko — Current Features

---

**Login & Signup** — Create a local account with your name, email and password. Each user's data is completely separate from everyone else's.

**Admin Login** — Log in with `admin` / `admin` to access the admin panel. No account needed, just those credentials.

**Admin Panel** — Admins can see every registered user, view their full transaction history and balance summary, edit their details, delete them (and all their data), or create brand new accounts from scratch.

**Home Dashboard** — The first thing you see when you open the app. Shows your current balance, total income vs expenses, a list of your 6 most recent transactions, your monthly goal progress bar, and your three streak counters.

**Add Transaction** — Tap the + button to log a transaction. Pick income or expense, choose a category, write a description, enter the amount. The date and time are captured automatically — no picking required.

**Edit & Delete Transactions** — Long press any transaction on the home screen to edit it or delete it.

**Expense Categories** — 18 built-in expense categories out of the box: food, groceries, coffee, alcohol, transport, fuel, shopping, clothing, entertainment, fitness, utilities, rent, medical, education, pets, travel, gifts, subscriptions.

**Income Categories** — 6 built-in income categories: salary, freelance, business, investment, gift, other. Income transactions have their own separate category picker.

**Custom Categories** — You can create your own categories for both income and expenses, with whatever name you want.

**Receipt Photos** — Attach a photo to any transaction. Take one with your camera or pick one from your gallery. Useful for receipts, warranties, anything you want to remember.

**Budget Tracking** — Set a spending limit on any category. The budget screen shows you how much you've spent vs your limit with a progress bar that changes colour as you get close.

**Monthly Goals** — Set a minimum and maximum spending target for the month. The home screen shows a live progress bar so you always know where you stand.

**Date Filter** — Filter all your transactions to a custom date range — handy for checking spending during a specific trip, event or period.

**Category Totals** — See exactly how much you've spent in each category over any date range. Good for spotting where your money actually goes.

**Reports & Analytics** — A pie chart showing your category split and a bar chart comparing income vs expenses side by side.

**Streaks** — Three streaks tracked on the home screen: how many days in a row you've opened the app, how many consecutive days without any spending, and how many months in a row you stayed under your monthly goal.

**Badges** — 10 earnable badges that unlock as you hit milestones. First transaction, 7-day login streak, 30-day login streak, 3 and 7 no-spend days, staying under budget for 1 and 3 months, 50 and 100 transactions logged, and saving R1 000 net in a single month. Each badge has an emoji icon and shows locked/earned state.

**Confetti** — A small confetti burst plays the first time you earn a badge or complete a challenge. It only fires once per achievement — not every time you open the page.

**Challenges** — Weekly and monthly spending challenges with live progress bars. Two new weekly challenges every Monday, plus the "No Alcohol This Month" challenge which is always pinned, alongside a rotating monthly challenge.

**Rewards Tab** — A dedicated tab in the bottom nav with two sections inside: Achievements (your challenges) and Badges.

**Profile Editing** — Tap your name on the Settings screen to update your first name and surname.

**Clear All Data** — A button in Settings that wipes all your transaction history and resets your balance to zero, after a confirmation prompt.

**Log Out** — Clears your session and returns to the login screen.
